Each page has a specific title and description, static HTML instructions, worked examples, relevant task links and a matching Markdown companion. Homepage quick-start links now expose these five tools plus the existing QR generator. Other tools, teaching resources and guides remain available.

Sources and observations are in search-evidence.json. Candidate phrases are hypotheses to test, not volume measurements. No new keyword-variant doorway pages were created.
